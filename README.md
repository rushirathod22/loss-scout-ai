# Loss Scout AI

You are a senior full-stack AI engineer, product designer, UX designer, and hackathon engineer. I am building an 8-hour hackathon MVP called: LOSSCOPE AI Tagline: "Find the losses you don't see." Core idea: Losscope is an AI-powered "Invisible Loss Discovery Engine" that analyzes business operational data and identifies hidden financial, inventory, time, and resource losses that the user may not realize are happening. IMPORTANT: This is a hackathon MVP. Prioritize: 1. Working core functionality 2. Excellent demo experience 3. Strong visual UI 4. Explainable AI 5. Fast implementation 6. Deployment readiness DO NOT over-engineer the system. DO NOT build unnecessary enterprise features. DO NOT create fake AI functionality where a normal rule would be sufficient. Every important AI insight must show supporting evidence. ================================================== 1. PRODUCT VISION ================================================== The user uploads or enters operational data. Losscope analyzes the data and discovers hidden patterns such as: - inventory wastage - over-purchasing - under-utilized inventory - unusual spending - recurring unnecessary expenses - delayed payments - delivery inefficiency - repeated operational work - abnormal demand patterns - potential resource wastage The system should answer: "What am I silently losing?" Then explain: 1. WHAT is being lost? 2. HOW MUCH is being lost? 3. WHY is it happening? 4. WHAT evidence supports the finding? 5. HOW confident is the system? 6. WHAT should the user do? 7. HOW MUCH could potentially be saved? The product must feel like an intelligent operational analyst rather than a chatbot. ================================================== 2. Create these detectors: A. Inventory Waste Detector Detect: waste_quantity > threshold Calculate: waste_cost = waste_quantity * unit_cost Aggregate by product. Return: product waste quantity waste cost frequency trend -------------------------------------------------- B. Overstock Detector Compare: average daily sales vs inventory remaining Flag products where: inventory remaining significantly exceeds expected demand. Calculate: excess inventory value -------------------------------------------------- C. Demand Mismatch Detector Compare: purchase quantity vs actual sales. Detect repeated over-purchasing. Example: Product: Chicken Sandwich Average demand: 42/day Average purchase: 60/day Excess: 18/day -------------------------------------------------- D. Delivery Inefficiency Detector Analyze delivery costs. Find: - unusually high delivery cost - repeated small deliveries - suppliers with high delivery cost ratio Calculate: delivery_cost / purchase_value -------------------------------------------------- E. Payment Delay Detector Analyze: payment_due_date payment_received_date Calculate: payment_delay_days Find recurring late payments. -------------------------------------------------- F. Trend Detector Compare: previous period vs current period. Detect: sales decrease waste increase cost increase margin decrease ================================================== 13.
